FAQ

Straight answers.

What do we have to do on our end?

Send us moments. Photos and short clips from activities, events, therapy wins, staff celebrations. One person on your team drops them in the shared folder. We handle the editing, writing, scheduling, posting, and your Google reviews.

What do you do with our Google Business Profile?

We keep the listing accurate (hours, phone, photos, services), monitor your reviews and respond where appropriate, in a way that protects resident privacy, and run a simple review-request process so happy families leave one. We never guarantee a review gets removed; Google decides that. We report the ones that break Google's rules and follow up.

How do you get access to our pages?

We send your page admin an access request through Meta Business, LinkedIn and Google; they click Accept. No passwords change hands. You remain the owner and can remove us in one click. Not sure who your admin is? We'll figure it out with you.

What about resident privacy and HIPAA?

We only post residents who have a signed photo/media consent on file with your facility, we never mention diagnoses or care details, and we never confirm who is a resident in comments or messages. Anything you ask us to remove comes down immediately.

How do we know it's working?

Every quarter you get a written report on reach, follower growth, engagement and what performed best.

Is there anything beyond the $500?

Not for the standard package. If you want something specific outside it, we quote it separately before doing anything.

Can we cancel?

Any time, from your billing portal. Service continues to the end of the paid month and your pages stay yours with everything we posted still on them.
